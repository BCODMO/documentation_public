# Internal Meetings & Agendas

## Rationale 

The goal of any meeting is to actively discuss topics and issues, their relevance to office goals and objectives, come to resolutions, and provide clarity on outcomes and actions.

**Meetings should be decision-focused rather than informational**. Purely informational topics are better distributed by email. 

The roles of Meeting Lead and note taker are to rotate each week to distribute responsibility across team members. 

All opinions, ideas, and information should be exhausted before determining a resolution \(which does not necessarily equate to consensus\). Resolutions should be clear and understood by all team members. Any action items should have designated champions and timelines. All topics resolve to a decision within one meeting time.  The following protocol is intended to provide a meeting framework.

## Lab Meetings

Regular weekly lab meetings take place on Thursdays at 10:00 AM. Lab meetings are led by the Director and Technical Director and typically address broader issues relevant to the whole office, including PIs and Co-PIs. Topics that are more specific to data management, data submissions, OSPREY, or Laminar are better addressed at a Monday [Data Manager's meeting](op_agenda.md#data-manager-meetings).

1. **Prior to Meeting:**
   1. Prioritization of agenda topics is completed by Director and Technical Director.
      1. Issues raised for meeting resolution should be documented in the [“BCO-DMO Agenda Topics” spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1cDwIKKJ7GV0TSXGqv9J0UiF4nfJrGy-VKR5me6iwnQM/edit#gid=0).
      2. An issue creator is identified per each agenda issue/topic.
   2. There will be one Meeting Lead who: 
      1. Finalizes the agenda,
      2. Keeps the meeting on track.
   3. There will be one note taker who: 
      1. Documents a high level summary of the discussion and any necessary info/links/etc.,
      2. Assigns action items within the notes.
   4. The Meeting Lead circulates the agenda at least 24 hr prior to meeting \(e.g. the Wednesday morning before a Thursday meeting\).
   5. Meeting Lead arrives 10 minutes early to ensure remote staff are on the call line and any projection or display is working and ready.
2. **Beginning of meeting:**
   1. Stand-ups at beginning of meeting \(first 10 minutes of meeting\):
      1. **One minute maximum per each team member**,
      2. Quickly describe your three main activities for the week,
      3. Note anything hanging up your progress.
   2. Use agenda to introduce issues/topics:
      1. Describes issues. Why is this important? What’s at stake?  
3. **Middle:**
   1. All ideas, opinions, and facts heard in open, frank discussion of the topic.
   2. Relevant discussion notes taken in Google by designated note-taker.
      1. Post-meeting, comments/suggested edits can be fielded in edit mode.
      2. Timed edit-mode may be used in Google.
4. **End:**
   1. Recap of all decisions \(last 5 min of meeting\):
      1. Capture decisions,
      2. Capture WHY we made those decisions,
      3. Designate and capture the “Designated Responsible Individual” \(DRI\) for each decision/action/etc.,
      4. Assign and capture due dates.
5. **Post-Meeting:**
   1. The meeting lead or note taker sends out the meeting recap/notes to attendees for comments \(in “Suggesting” Mode\). \(The lead and note taker should decide who's doing this.\)
   2. Action items should be sent out in the post-meeting follow-up email. Once an action is completed, the assignee will send out an email stating what they completed. The completed action items can then be crossed off the agenda \(select text → Format → Strikethrough\).
   3. Link the meeting notes to the [BCO-DMO Agenda Topics - Leaders/Rapper](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1cDwIKKJ7GV0TSXGqv9J0UiF4nfJrGy-VKR5me6iwnQM/edit#gid=1479734122) sheet.

## Data Manager Meetings 

Regular weekly Data Manager meetings take place on Mondays at 10:00 AM. These meeting should be focused on resolving any issues data managers are having with specific datasets, data types, PIs, data processing software, or SOPs. 

1. **Prior to Meeting:**
   1. Prioritization of agenda topics is completed by the meeting Lead.
      1. Issues raised for meeting resolution should be documented in the [“BCO-DMO Agenda Topics” spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1cDwIKKJ7GV0TSXGqv9J0UiF4nfJrGy-VKR5me6iwnQM/edit#gid=0).
      2. An issue creator is identified per each agenda issue/topic.
   2. There will be one Meeting Lead who: 
      1. Finalizes the agenda,
      2. Keeps the meeting on track.
   3. There will be one note taker who: 
      1. Documents a high level summary of the discussion and any necessary info/links/etc.,
      2. Assigns action items within the notes.
   4. The Meeting Lead circulates the agenda via email and/or Slack at least 24 hr prior to meeting \(e.g. the Friday before a Monday meeting\). Link the meeting notes to the [BCO-DMO Agenda Topics - Leaders/Rapper](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1cDwIKKJ7GV0TSXGqv9J0UiF4nfJrGy-VKR5me6iwnQM/edit#gid=1479734122) sheet.
   5. The issue creator should provide any relevant notes or links on the agenda. DMs should review this information before the meeting and come prepared.
   6. Meeting Lead arrives 10 minutes early to ensure remote staff are on the call line and any projection or display is working and ready.
2. **During the meeting:**
   1. Use agenda to introduce issues/topics:
      1. Describes issues. Why is this important? What’s at stake?  
      2. All ideas, opinions, and facts heard in open, frank discussion of the topic.
      3. Relevant discussion notes taken in Google by designated note-taker.
         1. Capture decisions,
         2. Capture WHY we made those decisions,
         3. Designate and capture the “Designated Responsible Individual” \(DRI\) for each decision/action/etc.,
         4. Assign and capture due dates.
3. **Post-Meeting:**
   1. The meeting lead or note taker sends out the meeting recap/notes to attendees for comments \(in “Suggesting” Mode\). \(The lead and note taker should decide who's doing this.\)
   2. Action items should be sent out in the post-meeting follow-up email. Once an action is completed, the assignee will send out an email stating what they completed. The completed action items can then be crossed off the agenda \(select text → Format → Strikethrough\).
