# Twitter

BCO-DMO has a Twitter account, [@BCODMO](https://twitter.com/BCODMO), the login credentials can be found in [Redmine knowledge base](https://kato.bco-dmo.org/issues/773). The account is primarly maintained by Tina Haskins, but anyone can contribute.

Hootsuite is one tool that can be used to schedule tweets in advance. To login, go to [https://www.hootsuite.com/](https://www.hootsuite.com/), choose "Log In" in the upper right-hand corner, and then choose "Use Social Sign In" at the bottom of the page. You can then log in using the BCO-DMO Twitter account credentials.
