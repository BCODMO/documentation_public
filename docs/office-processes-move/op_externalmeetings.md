## Rationale

Attendance and participation in external meetings, conferences, and workshops are critical to advancing the goals and objectives of the office. Engagement in communities of practice at relevant meetings and conferences provides opportunities for networking, outreach, collaboration, skills development and training, personal career growth, and keeps the office apprised of new advances in the field of data management.

Request form

In order to maximize benefit of external engagement to individual staff and the office, as well as coordinate staff time out of the office, please use the [travel request form/spreadsheet.](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScQQLl5CeAoNqDF94m7k1-eChOv4AfG6t7py9aoc321GykDFQ/viewform)

Please complete the form briefly describing how you will participate in the event, and provide 2-3 reasons your participation would benefit the office \(or your career in the case of training, etc.\). Requested travel will reviewed regularly to allow time for session/abstract submission, and early registrations.Requests
