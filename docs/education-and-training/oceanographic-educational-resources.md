# Oceanographic Educational Resources

* ## BIOS DataBytes:
  *   #### [Zooplankton vertical migration](https://bios.asu.edu/databytes/zooplankton-vertical-migration)

      Explore circadian rhythms as zooplankton make their daily journey up to the sea surface at night and back down to deeper waters during the day. This module links directly to the NSF-funded project in the BCO-DMO catalog. Explore the full project here: [_Collaborative Research: Diel physiological rhythms in a tropical oceanic copepod_](https://www.bco-dmo.org/project/764114)
