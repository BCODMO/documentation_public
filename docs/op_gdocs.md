# Google drive structure

Historical note: All of our original process documentation was moved or created on Gdrive by various team members. When we started using Google drive, we all had our own gmail accounts that we used to log in with. As people have left, we are not able to delete the files they created. Later, WHOI set up a corporate account that allows us to use our WHOI email address to sign in but those files are still under the control of the creator. We also have a BCO-DMO team drive that allows for anyone in the team to move or delete. To date, we have not made much use of that.
