We have been ordering giveaways for our booth. We strive to present the public to sustainable objects. So far we have used EcoPromotions as our supplier: [https://www.ecopromotionsonline.com/](https://www.ecopromotionsonline.com/). Our contact there is Michelle Carvalho \(michellec@ecopromotionsonline.com\). They already have our logo.
