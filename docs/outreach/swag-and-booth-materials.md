## Swag

We have been ordering giveaways for our booth. We strive to present the public with sustainable objects. So far we have used EcoPromotions as our supplier: [https://www.ecopromotionsonline.com/](https://www.ecopromotionsonline.com/). Our contact there is Michelle Carvalho \(michellec@ecopromotionsonline.com\). They already have our logo. Depending on how long the discussion about the logo goes, the actual production time for a certain product is about 2-3 weeks.

The logo that needs to be printed often can only be one color and in a certain format. The logo has been reworked in Inkscape. The logo that has been made for this purpose can be found in the logo folder, but should be remade with our new logo and improved font.
